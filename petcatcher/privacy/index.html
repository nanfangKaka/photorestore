Privacy Policy

PetCatcher · Last updated: August 2, 2026

Overview

PetCatcher is an iOS app that helps you photograph pets, identify species on device, generate collectible cards, and place them in AR. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

We do not require an account to use Guest mode. We do not sell personal data.

On-Device Processing

Species recognition, subject cutout, and motion analysis run on your iPhone using Apple frameworks (e.g. Vision / Core ML). Captured images are not uploaded to our servers for recognition in the current version of the App.

Photos, cutouts, and card images may be kept in the app’s private Documents folder so you can reopen your collection. Deleting a card in the app removes its media. Uninstalling the app removes local data.

Camera, AR & Recording

PetCatcher uses the camera for capture and for AR experiences (ARKit). Screen recording (ReplayKit), if you start it, stays on your device unless you choose to share the recording yourself.

Face Data

PetCatcher does not use Face ID, TrueDepth face maps, or biometric face templates for login or identification. If a photo you take happens to include a human face in the background, that imagery is treated like any other camera frame: processed on device for pet recognition / card creation and stored only locally as part of that capture. We do not build face identity profiles and do not sell face data.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — Payments and subscriptions
  • Sign in with Apple — Optional authentication (when you choose it)
  • Supabase — Optional cloud sync only if/when you (or a future release) enable a configured backend; not required for core offline play

When a third-party service is used, its own privacy policy also applies to data it processes.

Sharing

If you use the system Share sheet, you choose what to share and with whom. We do not upload your collection to social networks automatically.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.
