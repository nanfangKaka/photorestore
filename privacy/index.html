Overview

LumiRestore helps you enhance and colorize old photos using AI. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Third-Party Services

  • Replicate — AI image processing (GFPGAN for restoration, DeOldify for colorization)
  • Supabase — API gateway and usage limits
  • RevenueCat — Subscription management
  • Apple App Store — Payments

Photo Data

Photos are sent to our server only when you tap restore or colorize. They are transmitted over HTTPS, processed by AI models, and returned to your device. We do not use your photos to train models or for advertising.

On your device, LumiRestore may keep a limited local history of restored images (stored in the app’s private Documents folder) so you can reopen recent results. You can delete history items in the app. Uninstalling the app removes this local data.
